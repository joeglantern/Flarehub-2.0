# Mentorship System — Demo Guide

## Setup (before the demo)

You need 3 accounts:

| Role | Account |
|------|---------|
| Admin | Existing admin account |
| Mentor | A user with `isMentor: true` |
| Entrepreneur | Existing account with a completed profile |

To set `isMentor = true`: Admin → Users → open the mentor user → toggle isMentor on.

---

## Demo Flow

### Step 1 — Admin links mentor to program
1. Log in as **admin**
2. Go to **Mentor Management** in the sidebar
3. Click the mentor on the left
4. Click the **"Programs"** tab on the right panel
5. Click **"Add to program"** → select a program (e.g. YCIC) → confirm

> *"We now control which mentors are available per program."*

---

### Step 2 — Mentor generates invite link + QR code
1. Log in as the **mentor**
2. Go to **Invite Links** in the sidebar
3. See the YCIC program card → click **"Generate link"**
4. The unique URL appears with copy and QR buttons
5. Click **"QR Code"** to show the QR code modal

> *"Every mentor gets their own link. Share it on WhatsApp, print the QR at events, post it online — whatever works."*

**Copy the URL** — you'll need it for the next step.

---

### Step 3 — Entrepreneur applies
1. Open a different browser or incognito window
2. Log in as the **entrepreneur**
3. Paste the copied URL in the address bar
4. The apply page loads showing the mentor's name and program info
5. Write a short message → click **"Submit application"**

> *"This is what an entrepreneur sees when they scan the QR at a bootcamp or get the link from their community. One tap, done."*

---

### Step 4 — Mentor reviews and accepts
1. Go back to the **mentor** browser
2. Go to **Applications** in the sidebar
3. The new application appears with the entrepreneur's name, business stage, county, and their message
4. Click **"Accept"** → confirm

> *"Mentor sees who applied and their context. One click to accept or decline."*

---

### Step 5 — Entrepreneur sees the outcome
1. Go back to the **entrepreneur** browser
2. Go to **Mentorship** in the sidebar
3. Status shows **"Accepted"** with the mentor's name and a note that the mentor will be in touch

> *"The entrepreneur gets notified instantly and can always track where their application stands."*

---

## Key Talking Points

- **No manual back-and-forth** — the whole flow is self-serve
- **Program-scoped** — a mentor can be on multiple programs with a separate link for each
- **Mentor controls the link** — they can deactivate or regenerate it anytime
- **QR code** makes it work at in-person events, no app download needed
